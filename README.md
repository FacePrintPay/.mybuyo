EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

MyBuyO™ is a sovereign, biometric-authenticated CBDC payment platform that operates entirely without traditional banking intermediaries. It is not a crypto wallet, not a neobank, and not a payment processor in the conventional sense. It is a sovereign payment rail — owned and operated by FacePrintPay Inc. / Kre8tive Konceptz Ltd — that routes value directly between consenting parties using biometric identity verification and distributed ledger technology.

The platform's sovereign architecture means it operates independently of Visa, Mastercard, SWIFT, or any bank's goodwill. Users pay with their face. Transactions are confirmed on DLT. Settlement is instant. Privacy is protected by design. And the system connects directly to Federal Reserve networks, IRS Direct, SNAP/EBT rails, and cross-border mBridge infrastructure — making it the most complete sovereign payment stack ever built by a single independent team.

PRIMARY USE CASES

Use Case 1 — Consumer Contactless Payment

A consumer approaches a point of sale. They look at a camera. FacePrintPay verifies identity in 200ms. MyBuyO debits DigiDollar from their sovereign wallet. The merchant receives instant settlement. No card, no phone tap, no bank approval required. Transaction fee: <0.5% vs industry standard 2.9%.

Use Case 2 — Government Benefits (SNAP/EBT)

A SNAP beneficiary uses their biometric to access their monthly benefit balance. No EBT card required. No third-party processor taking a cut. Government funds flow directly to the citizen via the sovereign rail. IRS Direct integration means tax refunds land in the same sovereign wallet — no waiting for a check, no bank account required.

Use Case 3 — Cross-Border Payment (mBridge)

A US-based worker sends $500 to family abroad. Traditional wire: $35 fee, 3-5 days. Western Union: $45 fee. MyBuyO via mBridge-compatible rail: <$2 fee, instant settlement. The recipient receives DigiDollar equivalent in their sovereign wallet, convertible to local currency at the receiving end.

Use Case 4 — Enterprise B2B Smart Contract Payment

A Kre8tive Holdings portfolio company contracts with an enterprise client. Payment is escrowed in a smart contract triggered by delivery milestones. When the condition is met, payment auto-executes without invoicing, accounts payable, or bank wire delays. Disputes go to VideoCourts™ — another sovereign product in the stack.

Use Case 5 — C25 Agent Economy (Internal)

Within the CONSTELLATION25 ecosystem, Planetary Agents bill for services at $99/month per bioAuth key. Payments are processed through MyBuyO. The agent economy runs on the sovereign rail — no Stripe, no PayPal, no intermediary. FacePrintPay authentication gates access to agent compute.
